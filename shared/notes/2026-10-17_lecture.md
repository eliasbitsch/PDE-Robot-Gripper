# Lecture notes, Sat 17 Oct 2026

- FEM and analytical result may differ by at most **5 %** - compute both
  separately (independently), then compare.
- Scope: the whole **robot cell**, not just the gripper - gripper flanged onto
  the robot, robot in the cell with motion and grip analysis, safety fence,
  conveyor (buffer belt) and the part to be gripped.

Slides: "MultiPart in OnShape" (A. Probst, 44 slides, local copy under
`moodle/Onshape Multipart/`, not in the repo). Based on Onshape Learning
Center "Fundamentals: Multi-Part Part Studios". Two exercises:

1. **Master sketch** of a clamp (large arm, small arm, pins), fully defined.
2. **Multiple parts in one Part Studio** from that master sketch: extrudes as
   new parts, midplane reference plane, boolean subtract, mirror, fillets,
   material Aluminium 1060, then change the master sketch and check that all
   parts follow. Final check is the mass (quiz).

Task: do the exercise **per group in ONE Onshape document** and invite
`probst@technikum-wien.at` to it.

Why it matters for us: the same technique fits the gripper - one master
sketch for the four-bar drives jaws, links and body, so a change in V2 flows
through all parts.

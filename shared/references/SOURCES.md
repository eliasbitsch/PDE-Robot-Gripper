# Sources for third-party material

## cartesian-hand.png

Isometric view of the Cartesian Hand, cropped from Fig. 2b of:
B. Xia, B. Li, R. Shin, Z. Yang, J. Liu, B. Chen, "The Cartesian Hand:
In-Hand Manipulation with All-Linear Fingers", arXiv:2609.25696, Duke
University, 2026. Licence **CC BY 4.0**, used with attribution as the design
reference for the rack-and-pinion principle.

Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25696 ·
Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ktNcPPTPsQE

Key data used in the plan: every axis rack and pinion, Feetech servo with
1.39 N·m peak stall torque, ~170 N theoretical rack force, 52 mm gripper
stroke, ~60 mm/s, dovetail guides, whole 7-axis hand 850 g.

## Robotiq 2F-85 (data only)

Commercial electric parallel gripper, used as a force reference in the
gripping force estimate (V2) and as the four-bar variant in the comparison
(V1). Grip force 20-235 N, 24 V DC, stroke 85 mm, payload 5 kg.
Product page: https://robotiq.com/products/adaptive-grippers
